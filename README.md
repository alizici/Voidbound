## What is Voidbound?

Voidbound is a top-down space arcade combat game with a cultivation-style progression system. Pilot your ship through hostile orbits, mine ore, gather drones, defeat enemies, and ascend through ranks — from Mortal to Immortal — on the path of the Dao.

---

## How to play

### Controls

**iPhone / iPad (touch)**
- **Left side of screen** — virtual stick: move your ship
- **Right side of screen** — fire / abilities
- **Drag** — aim
- **Tap HUD icons** — open Hangar, Inventory, Map

**Mac**
- **WASD** or **arrow keys** — move
- **Mouse / trackpad** — aim
- **Click** or **spacebar** — fire
- **Tab** — open menus

### Core loop

1. **Mine ore** from asteroid fields — sell at the Hangar for currency
2. **Buy equipment** — guns, shields, drones, ammunition
3. **Hunt enemies** — earn battle rating (BR)
4. **Rank up** — every rank threshold unlocks new maps and gear tiers
5. **Survive harder maps** — bosses, elite mobs, drone swarms

### Save & sync

Progress saves automatically. With iCloud enabled on your device, your save syncs to all your Apple devices signed in to the same Apple ID. No account, no login.

## FAQ

**Q: Is there an account or login?**
No. The game has no servers. Everything runs on your device. iCloud sync (if enabled) is between your own Apple devices.

**Q: Is there in-app purchases or ads?**
No, not in this version. Single one-time purchase, no ads, no subscriptions.

**Q: My save disappeared after reinstalling — can I recover it?**
If iCloud was enabled when you played, your save is in your iCloud Key-Value store. Reinstall the app while signed in to the same Apple ID and it should restore within a few seconds. If iCloud was off, local saves are lost when the app is deleted.

**Q: The game crashes / freezes — what do I do?**
1. Force-quit the app and reopen
2. Restart your device
3. Make sure you're on the latest OS version
4. If it persists, email **alizi6969@gmail.com** with: device model, OS version, and what you were doing when it crashed

**Q: Can I play on Mac with a controller?**
Not in this version. Keyboard + mouse only on Mac. Controller support is planned.

**Q: How do I reset my game / start over?**
In the Hangar menu, there's a "Reset Save" option (under Settings). This wipes both local and iCloud data for this app. **There is no undo.**

**Q: Is there multiplayer?**
Not in this version. Voidbound is single-player only.

**Q: Why does the game ask for iCloud permission?**
Only to sync your save data between your own Apple devices. We never see this data — it lives in your private iCloud KV store. iCloud sync is optional; the game works fully without it.

**Q: What's the rank system?**
Battle rating (BR) accumulates from combat. Crossing certain BR thresholds promotes you to the next cultivation rank (Mortal → Foundation → Core → Nascent → Soul → Immortal). Each rank unlocks new maps, enemies, and equipment tiers.

**Q: I bought equipment but don't see it.**
Open the **Hangar** screen — newly bought equipment goes into your inventory. From there, drag or tap to equip it on your ship's slots.

## TL;DR

**Voidbound does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.** The game runs entirely on your device. Your save data is stored locally and (optionally) synced to your own iCloud account so you can continue your progress across your Apple devices. We never see it.

---

## What data the app handles

| Data | Where it lives | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Save data (ship, inventory, currency, rank, map progress) | Local device (`UserDefaults`) + your private iCloud Key-Value Store | So you don't lose your progress and can continue on another device signed in to the same Apple ID |
| App preferences (sound, settings) | Local device (`UserDefaults`) | Remember your choices between sessions |

That's the complete list. Nothing leaves your devices except through Apple's own iCloud sync — which is end-to-end controlled by Apple and tied to your Apple ID, not to us.

---

## What we do NOT collect

- No name, email, phone number, address, or any contact info
- No analytics, telemetry, crash reports, or usage statistics
- No advertising identifiers (IDFA), tracking pixels, or fingerprinting
- No location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, calendar, or health data
- No third-party SDKs (no Firebase, no Facebook SDK, no Unity Ads, nothing)
- No account system — no login, no registration, no password
- No in-app purchases (as of this version)
- No advertising

There are no servers operated by us. There is no backend.

---

## Third parties

- **Apple iCloud:** if iCloud Drive / iCloud Keychain is enabled on your device, Apple may sync your save data between your devices on your behalf. This is governed by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We do not have access to this data.
- **App Store:** the App Store provides us with anonymous, aggregated download counts and crash reports as part of their developer tools. This is standard for any iOS/macOS app and governed by Apple.

We use no other third parties.

---

## Children

Voidbound is rated for general audiences (no chat, no user-generated content, no contact features). It complies with COPPA by collecting **zero** information from anyone, including children.

---

## Your rights

Because we don't collect data, there is nothing for us to delete, export, or correct on our side. To remove all game data:

1. Delete the app from your device — local save data is removed
2. On iOS: Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Voidbound → Delete Data (removes the iCloud KV copy)

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL with a new "Effective date".
